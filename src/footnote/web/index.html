Ask the annual reports.

Footnote answers questions about the 10-K filings of fifteen US companies. Every figure is read from the filing, every calculation is done in code and checked again, and every answer cites the page it came from. When the filings do not support an answer, it says so.

This hosted page replays answers the system produced for the questions below. To ask your own questions, run it locally with make demo; the steps are in the repository.

      What it covers

      Companies: Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Nvidia, Intel, Cisco, Adobe, Coca-Cola, PepsiCo, Procter & Gamble, Nike, Costco and Caterpillar.

      Figures, fiscal 2021 onward: revenue, operating income, operating margin, net income, diluted EPS, operating cash flow, capital expenditure, free cash flow, long-term debt and employees.

      Text: Items 1, 1A, 7 and 7A of each company's three most recent annual reports.

      Not covered: forecasts, investment advice, currency conversion, and any company outside the list.

      Data from SEC EDGAR. Not investment advice.
